# Buttons you can add

Every button LittleLink comes with, in the order of its own list. Each one is a single line of HTML.

To add one to your page, copy its line into `index.html`, between `<nav class="button-stack" aria-label="Links">` and
`</nav>`, where the other buttons are. Then change `href="#"` to your own address, and the words just before `</a>` to
what the button should say. Its colours come from `css/brands.css` and its icon from `images/icons/`.

Or open the app in Tofu's builder and ask for it, for example: "Add a Spotify button that links to my artist page."
